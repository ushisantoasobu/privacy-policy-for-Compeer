**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to the Compeer app (hereby referred to as "Application") for iOS devices that was created by Shunsuke Sato (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

**Information Collection and Use**

The Application does not collect any personal information from users. It operates entirely on your device and does not transmit any data to external servers, the Service Provider, or any third parties.

The Application accesses audio files that you explicitly select through the system file picker. These files are referenced via Security-Scoped Bookmarks stored locally on your device and are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with anyone.

The following information is stored locally on your device only:

*   Names and timestamps of "comparisons" (collections of audio files that you create)
*   File names of the audio files you add to comparisons
*   File metadata such as duration and sample rate
*   Security-Scoped Bookmark data that allows the Application to access the files you have selected

The Application does not access:

*   Your location
*   Your microphone
*   Your camera
*   Your contacts, calendars, photos, or any other personal data
*   The network (the Application performs no network requests)

**Third Party Access**

The Application does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising networks, crash reporting services, or other SDKs. No information leaves your device.

**Opt-Out Rights**

You can stop all storage of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes available on your iOS device.

**Data Retention Policy**

All data handled by the Application is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the Application removes all of this data. The Service Provider does not retain any of your data because the Service Provider never receives it.

**Children**

The Application does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

**Security**

All data handled by the Application is stored locally on your device using Apple's standard iOS data protection mechanisms. The Service Provider does not have access to any of your data.

You are advised to consult this Privacy Policy regularly for any changes, as continued use is deemed approval of all changes.
